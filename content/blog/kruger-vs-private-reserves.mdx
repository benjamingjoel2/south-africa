It's the question we're asked most: *should we go to Kruger, or a private reserve?* The good news is that wildlife moves freely between them. The fences between Kruger and its neighbouring private reserves came down decades ago. The difference is how you experience it.

## Kruger National Park

Kruger is vast: nearly 20,000 km², with a network of tar and gravel roads, government rest camps and a handful of luxury concession lodges.

**Best for:** independent travellers, repeat visitors, self-drivers, and anyone on a tighter budget.

- **Self-drive freedom.** Explore at your own pace, with a picnic and a map.
- **Huge variety** of landscapes, from the southern bushveld to the northern mopane and baobabs.
- **Value.** Rest camps and mid-range lodges cost a fraction of private reserves.
- **But:** vehicles must stay on the roads, popular sightings can draw a queue of cars, and gate times are strict.

## The private reserves (Greater Kruger)

The Sabi Sand, Timbavati, Klaserie, Manyeleti and others border Kruger's western edge. They're privately managed, and lodges have exclusive traversing rights.

**Best for:** first-time safari-goers, honeymooners, photographers and anyone who wants the most intimate sightings.

- **Off-road tracking.** Rangers and trackers can follow animals into the bush.
- **Fewer vehicles.** Most reserves cap sightings at two or three vehicles.
- **All-inclusive.** Two guided activities a day, meals and (usually) drinks.
- **Walking safaris** are easy to add.
- **But:** it's significantly more expensive, and children often have minimum ages for game drives.

## Side by side

| | Kruger National Park | Private reserves |
|---|---|---|
| Guiding | Self-drive, or guided drives from camps | Private ranger + tracker |
| Off-road | No | Yes |
| Crowds at sightings | Can be busy | Limited vehicles |
| Night drives | Limited, booked separately | Included daily |
| Cost | $–$$$ | $$$–$$$$ |
| Kids | Very family-friendly | Varies by lodge |

## Our recommendation

For a **first safari**, we usually suggest **three or four nights in a private reserve**. The quality of guiding transforms the experience. For **longer trips or repeat visitors**, combining a self-drive in Kruger with a private-reserve finale gives you the best of both.
