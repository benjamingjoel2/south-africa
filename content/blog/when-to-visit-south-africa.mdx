South Africa is a year-round destination, but **the best time depends on where you're going**. Two things to remember:

- **Seasons are reversed** from Europe and North America. December is midsummer; July is midwinter.
- **The Cape and the north peak at different times.** Cape Town has a Mediterranean climate (dry summers, winter rain from June to August). Kruger, Johannesburg and KwaZulu-Natal have summer rainfall (roughly November to March) and dry, sunny winters, which are **peak game-viewing season from May to September**.

## Month by month

**January.** High summer. Cape Town is hot, dry and busy; book early. Kruger is green and lush with baby animals, but hot, with afternoon storms.

**February.** Arguably Cape Town's best month: warm sea, settled weather, and the Winelands harvest begins. Good value on safari.

**March.** Harvest season in the Winelands, warm days at the Cape, and the end of the summer rains in the north.

**April.** Autumn colours in the Winelands. The bush starts to dry out. A great all-round shoulder month, but watch for Easter holidays.

**May.** Crisp, clear days in Kruger as game viewing improves. Cape Town is quieter and good value, with occasional rain.

**June.** Winter. Peak safari conditions begin. Whale season starts on the Cape coast. Cape Town sees rain fronts between brilliant blue days.

**July.** Excellent safari month: dry, cool and great for sightings. Cape Town is green and cosy; whales in Hermanus. Mid-year school holidays bring families.

**August.** Still peak safari. Whales are plentiful. West Coast wildflowers begin to bloom north of Cape Town.

**September.** One of our favourite months anywhere in the country: spring at the Cape, prime whale watching and superb game viewing.

**October.** Peak whale season continues, Cape Town warms up and the bush is at its driest, so animals gather at water.

**November.** The first rains arrive in the north, bringing impala lambs and migrant birds. Lovely early summer at the Cape.

**December.** Festive season: South Africans head to the coast, and prices and demand peak. Book well ahead.

## At a glance

| Region | Best | Good | Quieter / mixed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cape Town & Winelands | Nov–Mar | Apr–May, Sep–Oct | Jun–Aug (rain) |
| Kruger & private reserves | May–Sep | Apr, Oct–Nov | Dec–Feb (heat, rain) |
| Whale coast (Hermanus) | Jun–Nov | Dec–Apr | — |
| Garden Route | Oct–Mar | Apr–May, Aug–Sep | Jun–Jul |
| KwaZulu-Natal coast | Apr–Sep | Oct–Nov, Mar | Dec–Feb (humid) |

## The short answer

For a classic **Cape Town and safari** combination, **September to November** and **March to May** balance good weather at the Cape with strong game viewing. Want summer at the beach? Go December to February and enjoy a lush, green safari. Prefer the very best wildlife? June to September, and pack a warm jacket for early drives.
